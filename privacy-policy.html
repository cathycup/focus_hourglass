Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Focus Hourglass. The app is designed to help you focus without collecting personal data.

Data Collection

Focus Hourglass does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell, rent, or share any personal data.

Local App Data

The app may store focus history, audio preferences, hourglass style, and app settings locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

Focus Hourglass does not require an account, does not use third-party analytics, does not show advertising, and does not use tracking technologies.

Support Email

If you contact us by email, your email client sends only the information you choose to include. We use that information only to respond to your request.

Data Deletion

Local app data can be removed by deleting the app from your device. If your operating system provides app data management settings, you may also use those settings to remove local data.

Children's Privacy

Because Focus Hourglass does not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of Focus Hourglass changes how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those changes are released.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at cathycup@163.com.
